# Part 14 – Juggernaut & Fortress Zero

- Added the Juggernaut heavy infected class with a distinct armored silhouette, high health, slow movement, and heavy melee damage.
- Juggernauts can appear in initial hordes at a very low rate and in waves from wave 5 onward.
- Added the Fortress Zero map: storm weather, hardened compound palette, five spawn lanes, and three capture sectors.
- Added the map to the campaign map rotation and expanded zombie/map regression test coverage.
- Blood presentation remains stylized and non-graphic.
