# Part 20 – Combined-Arms Visual Identity & Balance Pass

## Design direction
A unified near-future coastal-defense aesthetic: distinct silhouettes and roles for Vanguard infantry, Bulwark support, Longwatch marksmen, M-ATV/IFV/MBT vehicles, and Longbow/Rescue/Assault helicopters.

## Balance changes
- Infantry: small survivability/ammunition improvements to rifle, heavy, RPG, medic, and marksman roles.
- Ground vehicles: modest durability/ammunition improvements to the M-ATV, IFV, and MBT.
- Aircraft: modest durability/ammunition improvements to Apache, Blackhawk, Hind, Chinook, and MQ-9.
- Zombie roster: small adjustments to baseline shambler/sprinter/riot pressure.
- Boss roster: Juggernaut and Tank boss durability/damage increased slightly while Juggernaut speed reduced for readable counterplay.

## Intent
This is a role-based rebalance, not a literal 10× multiplier. A 10× damage/health increase would make the game unbalanced and could worsen mobile performance.

## Validation
- Archive integrity checked after packaging.
- Android APK build, TypeScript full typecheck, gameplay balance, and device FPS remain unverified.
